# SMR Lab Website — Design Ideas

## Approach 1: Industrial Precision
<response>
<text>
**Design Movement:** Bauhaus meets Industrial Engineering

**Core Principles:**
- Grid-based asymmetry with strong vertical rhythm
- High contrast between deep navy and warm off-white
- Technical precision expressed through sharp edges and monospaced type accents
- Data and numbers as visual elements

**Color Philosophy:**
- Background: Deep navy (#0B1120) — conveys authority and technical depth
- Primary accent: Electric blue (#2563EB) — signals innovation
- Secondary: Amber (#F59E0B) — warmth and energy for highlights
- Text: Warm off-white (#F8F5EE) — avoids harsh pure white

**Layout Paradigm:**
- Left-anchored navigation with a persistent sidebar on desktop
- Content sections use a 12-column grid with intentional asymmetric placement
- Hero section: full-width with a diagonal split between image and text

**Signature Elements:**
- Thin horizontal rule lines with circuit-board-like decorative nodes
- Monospaced font for labels and metadata (e.g., "01 / Research Areas")
- Subtle dot-grid background texture on hero sections

**Interaction Philosophy:**
- Hover states reveal underline animations from left to right
- Section transitions use a horizontal slide-in from the right

**Animation:**
- Entrance: elements fade in with a 20px upward translate, staggered 60ms
- Hover on cards: slight upward lift (translateY -4px) with shadow deepening

**Typography System:**
- Display: Space Grotesk Bold — geometric, technical
- Body: DM Sans Regular — clean and readable
- Accent/Labels: JetBrains Mono — monospaced for technical metadata
</text>

## Approach 2: Academic Clarity with Editorial Flair
<response>
<text>
**Design Movement:** Editorial Academic — inspired by Nature journal and MIT Media Lab

**Core Principles:**
- Clean white canvas with generous whitespace
- Typography as the primary design element
- Structured information hierarchy with clear visual levels
- Restrained use of color — one strong accent only

**Color Philosophy:**
- Background: Pure white (#FFFFFF) with light warm gray sections (#F7F6F3)
- Primary accent: Deep forest green (#1B4332) — academic gravitas
- Highlight: Saffron yellow (#FBBF24) — selective, never overused
- Text: Near-black (#111827) for body, medium gray for metadata

**Layout Paradigm:**
- Full-width top navigation with underline-only active states
- Two-column asymmetric layout: 65/35 split for content vs. sidebar info
- Publications section uses a newspaper-style multi-column grid

**Signature Elements:**
- Large pull-quote typography for key research stats
- Thin vertical divider lines separating content columns
- Section headers use oversized, light-weight display text

**Interaction Philosophy:**
- Minimal animation — content is the focus
- Smooth scroll with section highlight in navigation

**Animation:**
- Subtle fade-in on scroll (opacity 0→1, 300ms)
- No bounce or spring — purely linear or ease-out

**Typography System:**
- Display: Playfair Display — classic, editorial authority
- Body: Source Serif 4 — academic and readable
- UI/Labels: DM Sans — clean sans-serif for buttons and nav
</text>

## Approach 3: Futuristic Tech Lab (CHOSEN)
<response>
<text>
**Design Movement:** Deep Tech / Cyber-Physical Systems — inspired by CERN, NASA JPL, and MIT CSAIL lab sites

**Core Principles:**
- Dark background with luminous accent colors — conveys cutting-edge research
- Information-dense but breathable — like a technical dashboard
- Geometric precision with subtle organic curves for approachability
- Strong visual hierarchy through size contrast, not just color

**Color Philosophy:**
- Background: Very dark slate (#0A0F1E) — depth and focus
- Surface: Dark blue-gray (#111827 / #1E293B) — card backgrounds
- Primary accent: Cyan-blue (#06B6D4) — technology, precision
- Secondary accent: Violet (#7C3AED) — AI/ML research identity
- Text: Light gray (#E2E8F0) and white (#FFFFFF)
- Muted: Slate gray (#64748B) for secondary text

**Layout Paradigm:**
- Full-width sticky top navigation with frosted glass effect
- Hero: Split layout — left text block, right animated/visual element
- Research section: Horizontal card scroll with category tags
- Publications: Clean list with year badges and journal tags
- Team: Grid with hover-reveal bio cards

**Signature Elements:**
- Glowing border accents on cards (1px cyan glow on hover)
- Subtle animated gradient mesh in hero background
- Tag/badge system using pill shapes with colored borders

**Interaction Philosophy:**
- Cards lift and glow on hover
- Navigation links have a sliding underline from center
- Smooth scroll with active section tracking in nav

**Animation:**
- Hero text: staggered word-by-word fade-in, 80ms delay each
- Cards: translateY(-6px) + box-shadow deepening on hover, 200ms ease-out
- Page load: sequential section reveal from top to bottom

**Typography System:**
- Display: Syne Bold — geometric, futuristic, distinctive
- Body: Inter — clean and readable (only for body text, not headings)
- Accent/Labels: Space Mono — technical metadata and tags
</text>

## Selected Approach: **Approach 3 — Futuristic Tech Lab**

This design philosophy best represents the SMR Lab's identity as a cutting-edge research group working at the intersection of AI, robotics, and smart manufacturing. The dark, luminous aesthetic signals technical sophistication while remaining professional and academic.
